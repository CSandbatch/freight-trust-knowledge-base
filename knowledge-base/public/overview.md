# Freight Trust public materials

This library contains the programme materials that have been explicitly approved for
public reading. It is intentionally limited: working research, drafts, review material,
and internal records are not published here.

The materials describe a proposed research programme. They are not legal, regulatory, or
operational advice, and they do not report demonstrated programme outcomes.
