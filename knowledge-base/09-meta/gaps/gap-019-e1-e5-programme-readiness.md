# GAP-019 — E1-E5 is build-start-ready but not pilot-ready

## Gap

[[03-research-evidence/integrated-e1-e5-research-programme]] and
[[03-research-evidence/e1-e5-build-readiness-and-run-contract]] define the common dependency,
interface, run packet and build-start slices. Engineers may now create public/synthetic fixture
implementations without implying a result. The portfolio remains unrun and cannot pass `DG-P0`
or begin data-backed pilots until experiment-specific semantic/policy authority, source rights,
estimators, thresholds, privacy boundaries, implementations, owners and budgets are frozen.

## Experiment-specific closure

- **E1:** human semantic freeze, institutional/sponsor determination before reviewer-behavior
  research, real-case reviewer pilot, benchmark construction, source rights,
  exact estimators/operating point and executable C0–C3; C6 additionally closes [[gap-018-e1-llm-readiness]].
- **E2:** freight EPCIS profile, hidden truth/observability split, scoped anomaly task, privacy
  release model, frozen thresholds, explicit synthetic-versus-partner estimands, and an
  institutional/sponsor determination before its reviewer-performance experiment.
- **E3:** authority-backed policy oracle, one declared pinned NGAC/PML or XACML native Phase I
  lane (with separate fixtures and conditional parity checks if a second lane is added),
  authenticated PEP/PDP, every-attempt audit reconciliation, decision algebra and privacy controls.
- **E4:** institutional human-subjects determination, private row-level store, approved instruments,
  assignment/exposure map, recruited frame, budget and disclosure-control gate.
- **E5:** formal scenario/constraint model, regulatory HOS layer, solver validation, policy/stress
  factorial, uncertainty/adversary priors and actor-level non-inferiority gate.

## Acceptance evidence

1. Each experiment passes protocol quality gates `G0–G2` under [[experiment-protocol-standard]].
2. Every upstream/downstream exchange passes the versioned interface contract.
3. No raw participant, partner, restricted, adjudication or credential material is Git-tracked.
4. The Phase I work plan and budget distinguish committed, conditional and Phase II work.
5. An independent reviewer finds no Critical/Major coherence or claim-boundary defect.
