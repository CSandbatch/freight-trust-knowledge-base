# DEC-014 — BellHill applicant and working PI designation

## Decision

BellHill is the current programme client and intended NSF SBIR applicant. Russell Berry is the
working Principal Investigator and retains the concurrent Research & Knowledge Architecture
Lead role.

This decision supersedes the applicant and personnel designations recorded in `DEC-001`,
`DEC-002` and `DEC-010`. Those identifiers and dates remain part of the audit chronology, but
their former names are intentionally anonymized under the commissioning user's zero-occurrence
migration instruction.

This is an explicit human-authorized exception to the usual named-history preservation rule in
[[methodology]]. The event chronology and immutable IDs remain visible, the redacted mapping
remains recoverable from Git history, and no agent independently chose to erase provenance.

## Boundaries

- BellHill's exact registered name, legal form, ownership, affiliate status and small-business
  eligibility are not established.
- Russell Berry's PI employment, effort, work authorization, research-security posture,
  compensation and résumé evidence remain unverified.
- The two working titles describe one person and must not create two salary lines or duplicate
  effort in the budget.
- No external organization, event participant or researcher is a BellHill partner unless a
  written commitment is obtained and recorded.

## Consequences

- [[client-bellhill]] becomes the canonical client record.
- Applicant, team, budget, readiness, ownership and approval records use the new working state.
- `GAP-001` remains open and is rewritten around BellHill legal-form and Russell Berry PI
  eligibility evidence.
- Arkansas ecosystem research may identify outreach candidates but cannot establish endorsement,
  data access, a pilot relationship or market demand.
