# Task — E1-E5 Rabbit programme alignment

## Scope and inputs

Run the project-local Freight Trust research cycle across
[[03-research-evidence/experiment-e1-entity-resolution-and-identity-assurance]],
[[03-research-evidence/experiment-e2-facility-event-provenance-and-dwell-reconstruction]],
[[03-research-evidence/experiment-e3-federated-access-and-policy-enforcement]],
[[03-research-evidence/experiment-e4-participation-and-small-carrier-equity]], and
[[03-research-evidence/experiment-e5-orchestration-value]]. Treat E1 as the semantic and
benchmark anchor, and inspect the programme strategy, datasets, methods, source cards,
decision/gap records, SBIR drafts, and execution plan that consume these protocols.

## Required output

1. Rabbit-style primary/peer-reviewed source maps for E1, E2-E3, and E4-E5, including exact
   support, source class, access/publication date, limitations, conflicts, and negative findings.
2. One integrated experiment dependency graph and shared claim boundary.
3. Source-backed corrections and annotations in the canonical vault, with new unknowns recorded
   as atomic gaps or decisions rather than resolved by inference.
4. An independent adversarial review and a reproducible validation report.

## Acceptance gate

- Every changed factual proposition is entailed by an adjacent source or labeled as proposed,
  synthetic, partner-dependent, inferred, or unresolved.
- Conditions and identifiers retain experiment-local meaning; shared gates are qualified.
- Upstream outputs consumed downstream have matching names, versions, quality fields, and
  uncertainty semantics.
- No experiment result, production capability, partner commitment, or legal conclusion is
  claimed without evidence.
- Knowledge-base, unit, deterministic site, graph, and whitespace checks pass.

## Dependencies and escalation

Human approval remains required for legal conclusions, partner/data authorization, E1 semantic
freeze, numeric targets, SBIR route, consequential-use policy, and final-test opening. Research
may narrow those decisions but may not make them.
