## Findings and limits

- The acquired Census frame is reproducible and internally row-count consistent; this does not
  establish external completeness, daily delivery reliability or a redistribution right.
- Full-corpus missingness varies sharply. For example, legal name has 88 missing values, phone
  has 148,276, DBA name has 3,279,104, and business-organization description has 3,778,393.
- Text/sentinel values occur in fields whose names suggest narrower semantics. Raw evidence is
  therefore preserved and semantic coercion is avoided.
- The candidate pilot is capped and prefix-bounded. Its reduction ratio is not blocking recall,
  and its row-level cases are not gold labels.
- Shared name, address, phone, ZIP, insurer, owner or other relationship evidence never entails
  same legal person by itself.
