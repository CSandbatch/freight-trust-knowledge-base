## 0.9.1 E1 definition hardening

This point release executes Step 1 of E1 against current sources. It adds a source-grounded
carrier-identity standard, machine-readable ontology, 64-claim ledger, reviewer decision tree,
70-case adversarial suite, hostile evaluation, adjudicator/COI/training protocol, Louisiana/Texas
state-source access pilot, and a 27/27 structural conformance report. The old “same carrier” formulation is replaced
by layered legal-person identity, FMCSA registrant/identifier continuity, typed relationships, and
separate regulatory dispositions. RC1 is ready for PI/domain/counsel freeze review, not yet
represented as final legal or PI approval.

## Canonicality and caution

The active vault is the source of truth for this release. `08-archive/` is historical. SBIR
files under `04-sbir/drafts/` are working drafts, not submission-ready documents.

> **Internal working research asset.** This package contains draft proposal language,
> unresolved legal and commercial questions, research hypotheses, synthetic designs, and
> sources of varying verification status. Do not turn a working hypothesis, bracketed
> target, or internal legal/commercial analysis into a public claim without checking the
> evidence status and current control registers.

A documented hypothesis is not a demonstrated result. A primary source is not the same
thing as a successfully retrieved source. A named programme role is not, by itself, an
employment or budget commitment.
