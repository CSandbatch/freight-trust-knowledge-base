# FourKites — vendor product documentation

**Classification.** Architecture: **closed proprietary database; no knowledge-graph or
interoperability claim.** Primary focus: **detention/visibility**.

## Citation

FourKites. "Platform." <https://www.fourkites.com/platform/> — retrieved 2026-08-07.

## What the source establishes, in its own terms

Three stated architectural elements: a **"real-time network"** described as the
**"World's largest real-time supply chain network with 3M+ daily shipments"**;
**"continuously updated digital twins for predictive intelligence"** covering orders,
shipments, inventory, assets and facilities; and an **"AI-native agent platform"** branded
Loft.

The positioning statement, verbatim: **"While most control towers tell you what went wrong
yesterday, this one stops problems before they start."** Stated outcomes are preventing
stockouts, reducing costs, and automating execution.

Integration is described only at the deployment layer, verbatim: **"across any enterprise
system."**

## Axis-1 finding

No knowledge graph, formal ontology, semantic layer, shared vocabulary, or open-standard
claim appears on the page. "Digital twin" is an internal modelling construct over
FourKites' own ingested data, not a federation or interoperability mechanism. The page
gives no route by which an external party governs, contributes to, or queries the network
outside a FourKites commercial relationship.

## Limits and scope

Vendor self-description; not independent validation. The "3M+ daily shipments" figure is
company-stated with no measurement period, population definition, or method disclosed.
Page undated and subject to change.
