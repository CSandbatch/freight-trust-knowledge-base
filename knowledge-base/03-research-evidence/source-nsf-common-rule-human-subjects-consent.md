# NSF and Common Rule — human subjects and informed consent

## Citations

U.S. National Science Foundation. “Research Involving Human Subjects.”

<https://www.nsf.gov/funding/research-involving-human-subjects>

Electronic Code of Federal Regulations. 45 CFR 46.116, “General requirements for informed
consent,” current through 2026-08-17 when retrieved.

<https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/subtitle-A/subchapter-A/part-46/subpart-A/section-46.116>

## Supported propositions

NSF requires applicable human-subjects compliance and directs institutions to determine the
review category; while a review is pending, human-subjects work including recruitment may not
begin. The responsible institution/sponsor procedure determines who documents exemption or review
status; investigators do not self-authorize the study. The Common Rule requires legally effective,
voluntary consent in understandable language organized to facilitate comprehension, except where
an applicable waiver or alteration is approved.

## Limits

This card does not determine whether E4 is exempt or which review path applies. That decision
belongs to the responsible institution. A participant-facing study-record correction process is a
project governance choice unless another applicable rule or agreement creates a legal right.
