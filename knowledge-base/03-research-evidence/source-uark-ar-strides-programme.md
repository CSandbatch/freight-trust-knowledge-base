# University of Arkansas — AR-STRIDES programme

## Citation

Mack-Blackwell Transportation Center, University of Arkansas, *AR-STRIDES*,
<https://mack-blackwell.uark.edu/AR-STRIDES/>, retrieved 2026-09-03.

## What it establishes

The programme page describes a statewide NSF-funded research-capacity initiative led by the
University of Arkansas. It names four cores: transportation-data provisioning, geospatial AI,
secure cloud computing and synthetic supply-chain data. It also describes workforce development,
three research focus areas and the roles of the five consortium institutions.

## Conflict and limits

The page does not name core-project leads, milestones, datasets or outside partners. Its raw
HTML displays early-career seed grants as “up to 5,000” without a currency symbol. That rendering
does not support either a $5,000 or $75,000 ceiling; obtain programme guidelines before citing one.
