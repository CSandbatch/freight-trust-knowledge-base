# Re-identification risk in event logs

## Citation and verification

Rafiei et al., peer-reviewed open-access event-log privacy/re-identification study, 2020,
full text inspected 2026-08-18 at <https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7266437/>.

## Exact support

Supports the proposition that event traces can expose subjects through distinctive activity
sequences and linkage with auxiliary information; event-log privacy needs an explicit attacker
and disclosure model rather than identifier removal alone.

## Limits and E2 relevance

The study is not freight-specific and supplies no automatic release threshold. Freight subjects
may be persons, establishments, or commercially sensitive movements. E2 must define the
recipient, auxiliary knowledge, protected subject, linkage attack, risk metric, acceptance rule,
and utility measure for each release tier.
