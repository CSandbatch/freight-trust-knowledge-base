# Stochastic and strategic collaborative vehicle routing

## Citations

Jorge Oyola, Halvard Arntzen, and David L. Woodruff. “The stochastic vehicle routing problem,
a literature review, part I: models.” *EURO Journal on Transportation and Logistics* 7, 2018,
193–221.

<https://doi.org/10.1007/s13676-016-0100-5>

Margot Los et al. “Collaborative Vehicle Routing when Agents have Mixed Information Sharing
Attitudes.” *Transportation Research Procedia* 44, 2020, 94–101.

<https://doi.org/10.1016/j.trpro.2020.02.014>

## Supported propositions

Stochastic VRP is an established model family for uncertain inputs and recourse. Collaborative
routing literature also models heterogeneous willingness to share location, plan, and cost
information, providing precedent for separating reporting regimes from planning policies.

## Limits

These works do not provide empirical prevalence, effect sizes, or adversary parameters for the
target U.S. freight network. E5's distributions, actor knowledge, action sets, budgets, objectives,
and misreporting bounds remain predeclared modeling choices and unrun hypotheses.
