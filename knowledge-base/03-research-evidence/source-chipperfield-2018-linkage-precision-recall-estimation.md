# Chipperfield, Hansen & Rossiter 2018 — estimating linkage precision and recall

## Citation

James Chipperfield, Nathan Hansen, and Peter Rossiter. “Estimating Precision and Recall for Deterministic and Probabilistic Record Linkage.” *International Statistical Review* 86(2), 2018, 219–236. DOI: 10.1111/insr.12246.

<https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/insr.12246>

## Methodological contribution

The paper emphasizes that precision itself is uncertain even after clerical review and develops estimators for linkage precision/recall rather than treating benchmark counts as error-free population quantities.

## E1 consequence

E1 reports confidence intervals and reference-standard uncertainty rather than single point estimates. Final performance estimates are based on the benchmark sampling design and preserve uncertainty in adjudication.
