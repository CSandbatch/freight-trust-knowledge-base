# FMCSA 2024 broker-transparency proposed rule

## Citation

Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration, *Transparency in Property Broker Transactions*,
NPRM published 2024-11-20: <https://www.fmcsa.dot.gov/regulations/federal-register-documents/2024-27115>.

## Supported proposition

FMCSA proposed electronic broker transaction records and production within 48 hours after a
carrier request. The FMCSA rulemaking index inspected 2026-08-20 still listed the item as a
proposed rule.

## Limits

This is regulatory-status context, not current final law, an automatic-delivery requirement, a
Freight Trust correction right, a burden estimate or proof of OOIDA's preferred policy. The
negative final-rule retrieval is dated and must be refreshed before a legal or operational claim.
