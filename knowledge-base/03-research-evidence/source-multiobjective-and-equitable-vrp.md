# Multiobjective and equitable vehicle-routing models

## Citations

Nicolas Jozefowiez, Frédéric Semet, and El-Ghazali Talbi. “Multi-objective vehicle routing
problems.” *European Journal of Operational Research* 189(2), 2008, 293–309.

<https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ejor.2007.05.055>

Piotr Matl, Richard F. Hartl, and Thibaut Vidal. “Workload equity in vehicle routing: The impact
of alternative workload resources.” *Computers & Operations Research* 110, 2019, 116–129.

<https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cor.2019.05.016>

## Supported propositions

Multiobjective routing commonly reports nondominated trade-offs rather than one post hoc
composite. Equity results depend on both the workload resource and equity function; some
resource/function combinations produce undesirable optimization behavior.

## Limits

Pareto membership does not select a normative solution or show that a burden shift is acceptable.
These papers do not define a small-carrier fairness threshold. E5 must predeclare strata,
workload resource, actor-level deterioration statistics, and a veto or non-inferiority rule without
choosing numeric thresholds after results.
