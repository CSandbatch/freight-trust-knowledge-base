# GAO-16-401R — FMCSA small-business estimate

## Citation

U.S. Government Accountability Office. *Department of Transportation, Federal Motor
Carrier Safety Administration: Electronic Logging Devices and Hours of Service Supporting
Documents.* GAO-16-401R, 2016.

<https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-16-401r>

## Supported proposition

GAO reports FMCSA's estimate that approximately 99.1% of regulated motor carriers were small
businesses under the applicable Small Business Administration size standards used in that
rulemaking. GAO also states that regulated carriers span industries and no single SBA threshold
applies to all of them.

## Limits

This is a rulemaking estimate under heterogeneous revenue/employee standards. It is not an
owner-operator share, power-unit fleet distribution, measure of administrative capacity, or
estimate of disproportionate burden. E4 must obtain fleet bands from appropriate FMCSA data and
measure resources and burden directly.
