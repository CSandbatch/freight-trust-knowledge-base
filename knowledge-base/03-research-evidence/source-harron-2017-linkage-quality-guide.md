# Harron et al. — guide to evaluating linkage quality

## Citation

Katie Harron et al. “A guide to evaluating linkage quality for the analysis of linked data.” *International Journal of Epidemiology* 46(5), 2017, 1699–1710.

<https://academic.oup.com/ije/article/46/5/1699/4107250>

## Methodological contribution

The guide emphasizes precision/positive predictive value, sensitivity/recall, false-match rate, subgroup representativeness, and sensitivity analysis. Very low aggregate error can still create selection or measurement bias if errors concentrate in subgroups.

## E1 consequence

- subgroup performance is a validity check, not an optional fairness appendix;
- linkage/coverage rates and false-match rates are reported alongside precision/recall;
- sensitivity analyses vary thresholds and contested labels;
- aggregate accuracy cannot erase systematic error concentration.
