# Source — 24-entity coalition letter to DOJ on cargo-theft prosecution funding

This card exists to resolve three dangling wikilinks: [[source-ata-chameleon-carrier-position]],
[[source-tca-policy-positions]], and [[source-nastc-freight-fraud-white-paper]] each cited
`[[source-doj-cargo-theft-coalition-letter]]` before this card existed. The full retrieval and
reading of the letter was done under `GAP-008`'s NRF task and is recorded in complete detail in
[[source-nrf-organized-retail-crime-and-cargo-theft]] — this card is a pointer plus the summary
needed to stand on its own, not a duplicate transcription.

## Citation

Multi-association letter to Acting Attorney General Todd Blanche, U.S. Department of Justice,
**April 28, 2026**, with the FY2026 CJS appropriations report language enclosed. Hosted by ATA.
Retrieved 2026-08-08 from
<https://www.trucking.org/sites/default/files/2026-04/DOJ%20Letter%20-%20Cargo%20Theft%20Appropriations%20Implementation%20-%20FINAL.pdf>
(HTTP 200; PDF read directly, 5 pp.).

## What the source establishes

The letter urges DOJ to "fully implement the funding provided by Congress in the FY2026
Commerce, Justice, Science, and Related Agencies (CJS) Appropriations Act to establish dedicated
special prosecutors focused on combating supply chain fraud, cargo theft, organized retail crime
(ORC), and related financial fraud schemes, including gift card fraud." Its diagnosis, verbatim:
"Despite significant arrests by law enforcement, prosecutions frequently do not follow. State and
local prosecutors—already resource constrained—struggle to pursue complex, multijurisdictional
theft cases."

The enclosed FY2026 CJS report language (pp. 81–82) is itself a primary federal record: under
"Combatting Cargo Theft," the Committee directs DOJ to brief within 120 days on plans for
FBI-led regional task forces partnering with Homeland Security Investigations, TSA, and FMCSA,
and directs EOUSA to report within 90 days identifying the U.S. Attorney's Offices with the
highest cargo-theft crime rates under 18 U.S.C. §§659, 1991, and 2117.

**Complete signatory list, verbatim and in order (24 entities):**

American Property Casualty Insurance Association · American Trucking Associations · Association
of American Railroads · Association of Home Appliance Manufacturers · Auctane · Catalyst
Brands · DHL · GE Appliances, Haier · Intermodal Association of North America · International
Bridge, Inc. · Meat Import Council of America · Milwaukee Tool · National Auto Auction
Association · National Independent Auto Dealers Association · National Milk Producers
Federation · National Retail Federation · Recycled Materials Association · Retail Industry
Leaders Association · Supply Chain Federation · The Travelers Companies, Inc. · U.S. Chamber of
Commerce · U.S. Dairy Export Council · UPS · World Shipping Council.

Recorded in full so that absence claims about any body *not* on this list are checkable without
re-fetching the letter.

## Limits and scope

Every remedy the letter asks for is prosecutorial or federal-coordination in nature — special
prosecutors, task forces, inter-agency briefings. It does not ask for carrier vetting, identity
standards, or registration reform. It should never be cited as coalition support for this
programme's verification mechanism; it documents that 24 organizations, including ATA and NRF,
consider cargo theft a funded federal enforcement priority as of April 2026.
