# Staged Participation and Equity Evaluation

Tests concrete reciprocal offers using separate offer, accessibility-support, and optional
comprehension-support axes, measuring activation, retention, burden, appeals, and outcomes by
predeclared fleet-size band.

- Use: evaluate adoption incentives and small-carrier equity in E4.
- Strength: converts voluntary participation into a measurable hypothesis.
- Limitation: small samples and self-selection may limit causal conclusions.
- Assignment: invited organization is the provisional unit; cluster and exposure mappings must
  be frozen before randomization. Inadequate organizations/clusters makes the study
  feasibility-only rather than a powered causal test.
- Consent: lawful, understandable consent and no-paywall basic verification are common to every
  cell. Accessibility support is randomized independently of fleet stratum; comprehension support
  is additive and may not reduce required disclosure.
- Phase boundary: Phase I defaults to instrument validation, recruitment feasibility, burden
  measurement, and directional mechanism evidence. A powered field experiment requires the
  institutional determination, adequate precision, partner commitments, budget, and follow-up.
- Data boundary: participant-level records remain in private controlled storage; only
  disclosure-reviewed aggregates or synthetic examples enter the public knowledge base.
