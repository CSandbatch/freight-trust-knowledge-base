# Staged Participation and Equity Evaluation

Tests concrete reciprocal offers in stages, measuring activation, retention, burden, appeals, and outcomes by fleet-size band.

- Use: evaluate adoption incentives and small-carrier equity in E4.
- Strength: converts voluntary participation into a measurable hypothesis.
- Limitation: small samples and self-selection may limit causal conclusions.
