# Source — "NAIT" identified, and a confirmed absence

## The identification question, resolved

[[goals]] G6 lists "NAIT" without expansion, and the task brief raised two candidate
expansions: the National Association of Insurance and Financial Advisors, or the National
Association of Independent Truckers.

**In this context NAIT is the National Association of Independent Truckers.** The insurance
and financial advisors association exists but abbreviates as **NAIFA**, not NAIT, and has
no freight remit. NAIT is a trucking organization and is the only plausible referent on a
list otherwise composed of trucking and shipper bodies.

## Citation

National Association of Independent Truckers. *About NAIT*.
<https://www.naitusa.com/about-nait> (retrieved 2026-08-07, HTTP 200). Home:
<https://www.naitusa.com/>.

## What the source establishes

NAIT is a **member-benefits purchasing organization**, not an advocacy body.

Founded 1981 "to serve the needs of Independent Owner/Operators." Its stated primary
purpose, verbatim: "providing access to buying power through benefit programs delivering
options to reduce the cost of running a trucking business."

Activity is organized in four benefit categories: health and wellness, insurance products,
business tools, and entertainment/exclusive benefits. The organization operates through
partnerships to develop member benefits, with emphasis on group purchasing power and
member service.

## Negative finding — confirmed absence

**NAIT has no advocacy function and no position on any topic in this programme's scope.**
Its own About page contains no advocacy programme, no policy positions, no regulatory
comment activity, and no content on freight fraud, carrier identity verification, broker
transparency, cargo theft, detention, or dwell.

**Search scope, 2026-08-07:** naitusa.com About and home pages (both retrieved); web search
`"NAIT" trucking association freight what does NAIT stand for National Association
Independent Truckers`. No policy page, no comment filing, no position paper surfaced.

## Limits and scope

- The absence is scoped to NAIT's public website and one web search. A members-only
  newsletter position cannot be ruled out, but a body with no public advocacy apparatus is
  unlikely to hold one.
- NAIT's constituency — independent owner-operators — overlaps OOIDA's. OOIDA is the
  advocacy voice for that constituency; NAIT is the benefits vehicle. They should not be
  double-counted as two independent stakeholder positions.

## Consequence for the programme

NAIT should be **removed from the stakeholder matrix as a position-holder**. It may still
be relevant as a *distribution channel* to owner-operators — a benefits organization is a
plausible route to reach small carriers for the E4 participation and equity experiment —
but that is an outreach hypothesis, not a documented position, and it is a team decision
under [[goals]] non-goals.
