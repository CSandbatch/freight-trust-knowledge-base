# OOIDA — broker transaction-record access and timeliness

## Citation

Owner-Operator Independent Drivers Association. “OOIDA calls for stronger broker transparency
regs to protect small-business truckers.” 2025.

<https://www.ooida.com/2025/ooida-calls-for-stronger-broker-transparency-regs-to-protect-small-business-truckers/>

## Supported proposition

OOIDA states its support for electronic broker transaction records and a 48-hour response period
beginning when a carrier requests records, and it opposes contractual waiver of carrier access.
This verifies a first-party association position that record access and timeliness matter to its
constituency.

## Limits

This is advocacy by the association, not an independent estimate of small-carrier burden. It does
not establish a Freight Trust correction right, a correction-latency target, or that the proposed
system satisfies the requested broker-transparency rule. The official federal status is recorded
separately in [[source-fmcsa-2024-broker-transparency-nprm]]; the retrieved 48-hour requirement is
a proposal, not represented here as current law.
