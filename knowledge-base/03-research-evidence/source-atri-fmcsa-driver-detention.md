# ATRI and FMCSA driver-detention evidence boundary

## Citation and verification

American Transportation Research Institute, *New Research Documents Substantial Financial
and Safety Impacts from Truck Driver Detention*, 2024-09-04,
<https://truckingresearch.org/2024/09/new-research-documents-substantial-financial-and-safety-impacts-from-truck-driver-detention/>.
FMCSA, *Impact of Driver Detention Time on Safety and Operations*, current project page,
<https://www.fmcsa.dot.gov/research-and-analysis/impact-driver-detention-time-safety-and-operations>.
Inspected 2026-08-18.

## Exact support

ATRI reports survey-derived 2023 estimates: detention at 39.3% of stops, more than 135
million hours, $3.6 billion direct expense, and $11.5 billion lost productivity. FMCSA
treats detention measurement and safety/operations impacts as an active research problem.

## Limits and E2 relevance

ATRI's page summarizes its own industry research and does not define an E2 ground-truth
detention label. Aggregate cost estimates cannot label a specific dwell interval. E2 must
predeclare appointment, grace-period, responsibility, exception, and authority rules and
must report dwell separately when those predicates are unavailable.
