# Imai, Jiang, and Malani — interference and noncompliance

## Citation

Kosuke Imai, Zhichao Jiang, and Anup Malani. “Causal Inference with Interference and
Noncompliance in Two-Stage Randomized Experiments.” *Journal of the American Statistical
Association* 116(534), 2021, 632–644.

<https://imai.sites.fas.harvard.edu/research/spillover/>

## Supported proposition

Under a two-stage randomized design and the paper's stated stratified-interference and
noncompliance assumptions, direct and spillover effects for compliers can be identified and
estimated with randomization-based inference.

## Limits

The result does not make arbitrary network interference identifiable. E4 must freeze its
assignment unit, clusters, exposure mapping, estimands, and power assumptions. If the recruited
network cannot support the design, the study is feasibility-only.
