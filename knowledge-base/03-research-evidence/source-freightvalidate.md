# FreightValidate — vendor product documentation

**Classification.** Architecture: **closed proprietary verification database; no
knowledge-graph or interoperability claim.** Primary focus: **fraud detection** (carrier
and broker identity verification and vetting).

## Citation

FreightValidate. "About Us." <https://freightvalidate.com/aboutus> — retrieved 2026-08-07.

## What the source establishes, in its own terms

Self-description, verbatim: **"the premier Carrier & Freight Broker Identity Verification
and Vetting System serving Shippers, Carriers, Freight Brokers, Load-boards, and factoring
companies."**

Stated function, verbatim: it **"ensures regulatory compliance and swiftly identifies any
past, present, or potential fraudulent or double brokering threats within the industry."**

The output is a three-state label on an entity: **Validated** (no non-compliance reports),
**Warning** (potential fraud or compliance concerns), **Not Validated** (failed regulatory
requirements).

Founder named on the page: **Dale Prax**.

## Axis-1 finding

No knowledge graph, ontology, open standard, or data-sharing architecture appears anywhere
on the page. The three-state label is a proprietary determination, not a portable
credential.

## Vendor's own disclaimer — load-bearing

The page states that FreightValidate **"is not a substitute for comprehensive carrier,
broker and freight forwarder verification, monitoring, or evaluation"** and recommends
using it alongside other compliance resources. A vendor disclaiming sufficiency for the
task it markets is directly relevant to the programme's argument that no incumbent closes
the verification gap. It is quotable *as the vendor's own statement*.

## Limits and scope

Vendor self-description; not independent validation. "Premier" is unsupported marketing
language. No accuracy, coverage, false-positive, or false-negative figure is published.
