# Statistics Canada 2016 — clerical-review quality measurement

## Citation

Abel Dasylva, Melanie Abeysundera, Blache Akpoué, Mohammed Haddou, Abdelnasser Saïdi. “Measuring the Quality of a Probabilistic Linkage through Clerical-Reviews.” Statistics Canada International Methodology Symposium proceedings, 2016.

<https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/n1/en/catalogue/11-522-X201700014743>

## Methodological contribution

The Statistics Canada framework uses probability samples of record pairs, repeated independent reviews of some pairs, and latent-class analysis to acknowledge clerical-review error rather than treating every reviewer decision as infallible.

## E1 consequence

The primary benchmark keeps independent duplicate review and adjudication. If disagreement remains nontrivial after training, E1 estimates reference-standard uncertainty and may add a latent-class sensitivity analysis instead of silently treating adjudication as perfect truth.
