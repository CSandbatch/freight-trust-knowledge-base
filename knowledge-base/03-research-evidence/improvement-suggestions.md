# Ten Improvements to Strengthen the Project

1. Narrow the first buyer and workflow to carrier onboarding plus one facility-event dispute process; keep network-wide orchestration as the later application.
2. Replace every broad outcome promise with a baseline, intervention, threshold, and failure condition.
3. Make the benchmark a named Phase I deliverable so the project produces a reusable asset even if the product hypothesis fails.
4. Separate authoritative identity evidence, operational event evidence, and inferred risk indicators in both the data model and the pitch.
5. Add a formal abstention and human-review path; never equate a model score with a legal or safety determination.
6. Put small-carrier equity into the primary evaluation design, with fleet-size subgroup metrics, not a general fairness paragraph.
7. Lead with the neutral, federated, challengeable infrastructure distinction and explicitly disclose the conflict-of-interest risk in vendor-authored standards.
8. Secure one real partner and one permissioned event source early; use synthetic data for feasibility, not as a substitute for all external validation.
9. Turn participation into an experiment with concrete reciprocal benefits, measured burden, retention, and reasons for refusal.
10. Add a proposal-readiness gate: current solicitation facts, applicant eligibility, PI, data rights, partner letters, and numeric Phase I targets must be resolved before submission.
