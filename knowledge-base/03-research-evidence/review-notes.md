# Review Notes — Terra/Luna Wide-Net Pass

## R-WN-01 — Outcome overreach

- **Severity:** high
- **Claim IDs:** programme thesis; fraud/detention/empty-mile outcome language
- **Finding:** available sources establish a material operational problem and technical
  plausibility, not that a freight trust graph causes industry-wide reduction in fraud,
  detention, or empty miles.
- **Required action:** use the pilot hypotheses in `03-research-evidence/luna-wide-net-synthesis.md`; state
  downstream outcome reduction as a research question.
- **Route:** synthesis
- **Status:** resolved in programme section 7A; continue enforcing in publishing.

## R-WN-02 — Adoption mechanism undefined

- **Severity:** high
- **Claim IDs:** G7, governance/incentives architecture
- **Finding:** federation and neutrality do not themselves create willingness to share
  commercially sensitive data.
- **Required action:** predefine three reciprocal offers and measure participation,
  retention, requested data fields, and rejection reasons by stakeholder/fleet segment.
- **Route:** Luna adoption → Review
- **Status:** open; G12.

## R-WN-03 — Automated-score liability risk

- **Severity:** high
- **Claim IDs:** Freight Trust Index
- **Finding:** a composite score can create opaque, contestable consequences for carriers
  and may be mistaken for a legal standard of care.
- **Required action:** use evidence paths and optional challengeable indicators; require
  abstention, human review, correction, and appeal before consequential use.
- **Route:** Terra → legal/human review
- **Status:** open; G13.

## R-WN-04 — Evaluation dataset absent

- **Severity:** high
- **Claim IDs:** technical architecture and pilot claims
- **Finding:** no adjudicated U.S. freight benchmark was located for identity resolution
  and facility-event provenance.
- **Required action:** create a labeled benchmark and preregister precision, recall,
  calibration, abstention, completeness, and dispute-time measures.
- **Route:** Luna technical/operations
- **Status:** open; G14.

## R-WN-05 — Small-carrier equity remains inferential

- **Severity:** medium
- **Claim IDs:** G8
- **Finding:** public evidence supports small-carrier relevance and broker-transparency
  interest, but not the programme's specific estimate of compliance burden.
- **Required action:** conduct direct primary research with a documented sampling plan;
  segment pilot outcomes by fleet size.
- **Route:** Luna equity → human/team
- **Status:** open.

## R-WN-06 — Fraud cost range needs source-quality review

- **Severity:** medium
- **Claim IDs:** core-thesis $7–16B/year fraud claim
- **Finding:** the range has not been independently validated in this pass against an
  authoritative methodology.
- **Required action:** retain only as contextual and label the source/method before use;
  do not make it a load-bearing SBIR claim.
- **Route:** Rabbit quantitative
- **Status:** open.
