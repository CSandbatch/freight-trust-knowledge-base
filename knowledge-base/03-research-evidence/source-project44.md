## What the source establishes, in its own terms

The platform page describes a four-layer "Decision Intelligence Platform" — Connect, See,
Act, Automate — organised around a **"live data fabric"** and an **"API-first
architecture"** with **"5,000+ API integrations"** to carriers and to ERP, WMS, TMS and OMS
systems. The stated core function is real-time shipment visibility: tracking across modes,
exception management for at-risk shipments, and **detention & demurrage** optimisation.

The press release is where the graph language lives. Verbatim:

> "the world's largest real-time logistics data graph"

> "grounded in the world's largest, most accurate and real-time logistics data graph"

with the accompanying scale figures, verbatim: **"connects more than 259,000 carriers
across 186 countries and territories"**, **"processes over 700 million logistics events
daily"**, **"connects over 1.5 billion shipments annually"**, and a **"preferred carrier
network of 282,000 carriers"**.

## Why this is still the closed-proprietary side of axis 1

"Data graph" here is a scale-and-coverage claim about project44's own aggregated store. On
neither page does project44 state an ontology, a semantic layer, a shared vocabulary, an
RDF/property-graph model, a standards body, or any mechanism by which a party outside
project44's commercial relationships could query, contribute to, or federate with the
graph. The superlative framing — "world's largest", "a decade of" data aggregation —
positions the asset as a competitive moat. API integration is connectivity into a
single-vendor store, which is not the same property as interoperability between
independently governed stores.

This is a distinction the programme depends on, so it is stated rather than assumed: the
graph claim is real marketing language and should be quoted accurately, and it does not
constitute a knowledge-graph architecture in the sense G4 is testing.

## Limits and scope

Vendor self-description. Never independent market validation. No third-party audit of the
259,000-carrier, 1.5-billion-shipment, or 700-million-events figures was sought or found;
they are company-stated, undated as to measurement period, and of unstated method. The
platform page is undated and subject to change without notice — hence `review_by`.
