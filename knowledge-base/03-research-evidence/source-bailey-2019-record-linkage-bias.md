# Bailey et al. — linkage error can be systematic, not random

## Citation

Martha Bailey, Connor Cole, Morgan Henderson, Catherine Massey. “How Well Do Automated Linking Methods Perform? Lessons from U.S. Historical Data.” NBER Working Paper 24019, revised 2019.

<https://www.nber.org/papers/w24019>

## Methodological contribution

Across historical linkage settings, automated and hand-linking methods produced substantial false links and nonrepresentative linked samples; errors were systematically related to observed characteristics. The methodological implication is that linkage error can induce structured bias even where aggregate performance appears acceptable.

## E1 consequence

E1 predeclares error slices by fleet size, collision context, source availability, record age and jurisdiction, reports uncertainty for each, and does not interpret an aggregate score as evidence that deployment risk is uniformly low.
