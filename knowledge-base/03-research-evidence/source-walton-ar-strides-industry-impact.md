# Walton College report — AR-STRIDES industry engagement

## Citation

Sam M. Walton College of Business, *Connecting Research, Industry and Impact*, 2025–26
Dean's Report, <https://walton.uark.edu/report/connecting-research-industry-and-impact.php>,
retrieved 2026-09-03.

## What it establishes

The report identifies Janeth Gabaldon as AR-STRIDES Industry Engagement Lead and Walton lead
researcher and describes an industry-facing role for the work.

## Conflict and limits

The report calls AR-STRIDES an $8 million NSF initiative, conflicting with the federal award
record's $7 million estimated total. This is the institution's characterization, not the
controlling federal award amount. It does not establish BellHill access or interest.
