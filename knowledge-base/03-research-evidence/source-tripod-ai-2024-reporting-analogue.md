# TRIPOD+AI 2024 — transparent prediction-model reporting analogue

## Citation

Gary S. Collins et al. “TRIPOD+AI statement: updated guidance for reporting clinical prediction models that use regression or machine learning methods.” *BMJ* 385, 2024, e078378.

<https://www.bmj.com/content/385/bmj-2023-078378>

## Relevance and boundary

TRIPOD+AI is a clinical prediction-model reporting guideline, not an entity-resolution standard. It is used here only as a mature analogue for transparent reporting of data sources, sample selection, model development, validation, missingness, performance, calibration, subgroup results, and limitations.

## E1 consequence

The E1 reporting checklist explicitly requires enough detail to reproduce the sampling frame, preprocessing, feature availability time, tuning, model selection, validation cohort, calibration, uncertainty and subgroup analyses. No clinical claims are imported.
