# Ismael et al. — modeling external and empty freight trips

## Citation

Abdelrahman Ismael, Yantao Huang, Hui Shen, and Olcay Sahin. “Modeling External and Empty
Freight Truck Trips using Agent-Based Simulation.” *Procedia Computer Science* 238, 2024,
791–796. DOI 10.1016/j.procs.2024.06.093.

<https://doi.org/10.1016/j.procs.2024.06.093>

## Supported proposition

The paper is a published precedent for modeling external and empty truck movements with an
agent-based framework. It uses FAF and HPMS data with POLARIS and applies the model to the Austin,
Texas metropolitan area.

## Limits

This short conference-proceedings study does not validate Freight Trust, the E5 state model,
nationwide external validity, E5's strategic actors, or a causal claim that evidence federation
reduces empty miles. Its reported link-volume fit does not establish correct load-level empty/full
classification or actor-level economic effects.
