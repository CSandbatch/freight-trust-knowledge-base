# Arkansas State Chamber — 2025 Manufacturing Showcase report

## Citation

Arkansas State Chamber of Commerce/AIA, *2025 Annual Report*, released 2026,
<https://arkansasstatechamber.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/2025-Annual-Report-3.pdf>,
retrieved 2026-09-03.

## What it establishes

The retrospective report confirms that the inaugural October 2025 showcase occurred. It reports
450 participants and 85 exhibitors and says programming addressed supply-chain resilience,
automation, IoT and AI.

## Limits

Attendance and programme outcomes are organizer-reported and not independently audited. They do
not establish freight-market demand or BellHill interest.
