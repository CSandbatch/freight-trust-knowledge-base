# SAHPMM 2026 — AI disruption in supply-chain management

## Citation

Arkansas Hospital Association/SAHPMM, 2026 Annual Meeting event record and official agenda,
<https://www.arkhospitals.org/AAMSS/iCore/Events/Event_Display.aspx?EventKey=SAHPMM0426&WebsiteKey=1dd3fc9e-7f54-4ad3-8b0c-6c6675508e7a> and
<https://www.arkhospitals.org/images/webdocs/SAHPMM2026/AnnualMeeting2026.pdf>, retrieved
2026-09-03.

## What it establishes

The official records identify a 2026-04-17 meeting at DoubleTree Hot Springs and a session titled
“AI Disruption in Supply Chain Management,” led by Naeem Bajwa. The agenda covers forecasting,
procurement, inventory optimization, risks and AI-enabled orchestration.

## Limits

This is healthcare purchasing/materials management, not general freight. Attendance and outcomes
were not reported. Do not use it as freight-demand evidence.
