## Reproducible Conway scan

- **Window:** 2024-01-01 through 2026-09-03.
- **Targets:** UCA event, conference and College of Business pages plus Conway-oriented
  chamber/city results.
- **Query concepts:** `AI`, `artificial intelligence`, `logistics`, `supply chain`,
  `conference`, `meeting`, and `Conway`, used in pairwise combinations.
- **Inclusion rule:** an official page must connect AI to logistics or supply-chain content in
  the same event.
- **Near-match:** the 2024 IEEE Arkansas meeting joined a general AI panel and a separate
  Westrock Coffee tour, but did not connect them; [[source-ieee-arkansas-2024-conway]].
- **Result:** no qualifying Conway event was located within this scan. This is not a claim that
  none existed.
