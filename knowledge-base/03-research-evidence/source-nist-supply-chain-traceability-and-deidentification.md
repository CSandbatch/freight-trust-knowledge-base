# NIST traceability draft and de-identification guidance

## Citation and verification

NIST IR 8536, *Supply Chain Traceability: Manufacturing Meta-Framework*, Second Public
Draft, 2025, <https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/ir/2025/NIST.IR.8536.2pd.pdf>; current
project/status page <https://www.nccoe.nist.gov/projects/supply-chain-traceability-principles-manufacturing-meta-framework>.
NIST SP 800-188, *De-Identifying Government Datasets*, final 2023,
<https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/188/final>. Inspected 2026-08-18.

## Exact support

IR 8536 2PD provides a candidate manufacturing traceability meta-framework and records its
own draft status. SP 800-188 provides risk-based de-identification terminology and process
guidance, including release context and re-identification risk.

## Limits and E2/E3 relevance

IR 8536 is a second public draft, not final guidance; the initial draft is obsolete. Neither
source validates this freight architecture or supplies performance thresholds. SP 800-188
does not make a release anonymous by applying a named transformation.
