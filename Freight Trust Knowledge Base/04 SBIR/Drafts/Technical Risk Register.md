# Technical Risk Register

*Material risk register for the Phase I R&D programme (entity resolution, event provenance, governed federation), built to satisfy the proposal-control expectation in [[NSF SBIR STTR Process and Readiness Guide#8. What can be done now, before an invitation]] to "create a technical risk register" covering "data sparsity, adversarial manipulation, cross-party identity error, privacy leakage, bias/disparate impact, integration, and adoption risk—with a Phase I experiment attached to each material risk." Programme and hypothesis context: [[01 Client Briefs/Freight Trust Client Master Brief#5. Research goals and client deliverables]].*

## How to read this register

Each risk lists: **Impact** (what happens if unaddressed), **Likelihood note** (qualitative, since no company-specific base-rate data exists yet), the **Phase I experiment/control** that tests or mitigates it, its **go/no-go linkage** (which milestone or hypothesis in [[NSF SBIR STTR Process and Readiness Guide#5. Full Phase I proposal: build around proof, not aspiration]] or [[01 Client Briefs/Freight Trust Client Master Brief#5. Research goals and client deliverables]] it feeds), and an **owner-role placeholder** (no named individuals are assumed).
