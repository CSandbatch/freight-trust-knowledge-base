# Phase I Budget and Justification Draft

*Skeleton budget for a 12-month NSF SBIR Phase I award under NSF 26-510 (up to $305,000, 6–18 months performance). Every rate, salary, and headcount figure below is a placeholder. This document is a structure and a justification logic, not a submittable budget. See [[NSF SBIR STTR Process and Readiness Guide]] for the eligibility, allocation, and proposal-production rules this budget must satisfy, and [[01 Client Briefs/Freight Trust Client Master Brief]] for the underlying R&D aims this spend supports.*

## Line-item justification prose

**A. Senior personnel — PI.** The PI's effort funds direct technical leadership of all three research aims: designing the entity-resolution benchmark and calibration protocol (Aim 1), specifying the event-provenance schema and tamper/contradiction test design (Aim 2), and directing the federation policy-enforcement prototype (Aim 3). This is R&D leadership time, not administrative overhead, and must be consistent with NSF's PI employment rule (≥51% employed by the small business at award and throughout performance; see [[NSF SBIR STTR Process and Readiness Guide#2. Eligibility gate: resolve before writing at length]]).

**A. Senior personnel — other key personnel.** [ROLE PLACEHOLDER, e.g., security/data lead] time funds the privacy-preserving federation design and threat model underlying Aim 3; [ROLE PLACEHOLDER, e.g., commercial/product lead] time funds the customer-discovery and pilot-interest evidence gathering that Commercial Potential review requires (see [[Commercialization Plan Draft]]). Only named, real personnel should appear here at proposal time.

**B. Other personnel.** Research engineer/data-scientist effort funds the actual construction of the identity-resolution benchmark corpus, baseline models, calibration and segment-level error analysis (Aim 1), and the event-provenance/tamper-detection test harness (Aim 2). This is the largest technically necessary labor line because Phase I's core deliverable is a working, evaluated prototype and benchmark, not a report.

**C. Fringe benefits.** Fringe is a mandatory pass-through cost on all salaried labor (A + B) at the company's actual or federally negotiated fringe rate; it is not discretionary and must use the company's real rate, not an assumed one.

**D. Equipment.** Phase I R&D as scoped (benchmarking, prototyping, discovery) is not expected to require capital equipment above the NSF/company capitalization threshold; compute and storage needs are budgeted as Other Direct Costs (cloud services) instead. If a specific capital item is later identified, it must be justified individually against the threshold.

**E. Travel — customer discovery.** Travel funds in-person structured discovery interviews and pilot-interest conversations with broker/carrier network contacts, consistent with the Market Opportunity and Commercial Potential expectations in [[NSF SBIR STTR Process and Readiness Guide#3. Project Pitch: the required first submission]]. This directly produces the "commercial evidence still required" artifacts listed in [[Commercialization Plan Draft]].

## Two-thirds small-business R&D share check (SBIR)

## Technical and Business Assistance (TABA) — budgeted inside the $305,000 base

Per `SBIR Evidence Refresh.md` (verified directly against the nsf.gov NSF 26-510 solicitation page, which states the $305,000 figure "encompasses all direct and indirect costs as well as the small business fee, [TABA], and the optional...
