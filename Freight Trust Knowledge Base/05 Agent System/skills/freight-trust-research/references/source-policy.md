# Source Policy

Use primary source material for legal, regulatory, standards, public-programme, and
official-statistics claims. Use peer-reviewed studies to support technical feasibility,
measurement design, and organizational mechanisms. Treat vendor pages as statements of a
vendor's own product, never as independent market validation.

For time-sensitive legal, regulatory, solicitation, and market claims, record an access
date and recheck before external publication. Preserve prior evidence as dated history;
do not overwrite it silently. If a primary source cannot be obtained, label the claim
secondary-only or unverified.

For quantitative claims, state population, period, method, and whether the number is an
estimate, survey, administrative record, or modeled result. Do not combine figures from
different populations or years without explaining the conversion.
